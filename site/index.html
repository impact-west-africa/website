Why we exist

Our mission is to point people to the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Imagine living your entire life without ever hearing the gospel explained in a way you could understand. For millions of people across West Africa, that is still reality. In Senegal, where we serve, more than 95% of the population is Muslim — deeply spiritual people who recognize their own sinfulness and their need for forgiveness. Jesus is respected as a prophet, but most have never heard the biblical message of His death and resurrection, and the salvation He offers through faith in Him.

"And how can they believe in him if they have never heard about him? And how can they hear about him unless someone tells them?"Romans 10:14

Agricultural Development

Pointing farmers to the Master Farmer and His ways through applying biblical principles to agriculture and improve food security, equipping them to be good stewards of the resources God has given them and helping them find an abundant life in Christ

02

Medical Care

Caring for patients at a rural clinic where many arrive on foot or by donkey cart, treating what local resources cannot reach — and offering the hope of Christ to every patient and family.

03

Gospel Transformation

Walking alongside the people we serve, building trust and respect, so we gain the privilege of sharing Jesus with them and discipling them to become true followers of Christ.
