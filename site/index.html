Why we exist

Senegal

Map: mapswire.com (CC BY)

Our mission is to point people to Jesus Christ.

Imagine living your entire life without ever hearing the Gospel explained in a way you could understand. For millions across West Africa, that is still reality.

In Senegal, more than 95% of the population is Muslim. Many Muslims are kind, deeply spiritual people who believe in a higher power and recognize their need for forgiveness. Yet, although Jesus is respected as a prophet in Islam, most have never heard the biblical message of His death and resurrection or the salvation He offers through faith in Him.

Romans 10:14 asks, "And how can they believe in him if they have never heard about him? And how can they hear about him unless someone tells them?"

That's why we're there. We long for people to know Jesus personally and experience the hope, forgiveness, and new life found in Him.

We're Tom and Suja Brane, serving in West Africa since 2013.

Our strategy

Through agricultural development and medical care, we come alongside communities to meet real needs. As we build relationships of trust and respect, we have the privilege of sharing Jesus and the hope He offers.

Step one

Meet a real, felt need

Better harvests. Medical care that would otherwise be out of reach.

Step two

Build trust and respect

We stay, we return, and we show up in the hard moments alongside the people we serve.

Step three

Share Jesus

So they have the same opportunity each of us has to accept Jesus as Lord and Savior.

Step four

See lives transformed

Watching God transform lives physically and spiritually.
