Pray with Us

We believe prayer is an essential part of the work God is doing in West Africa. We ask Him to open doors among the unreached, provide good harvests, bring healing to patients in our clinic, and equip local believers to teach and train others in their own nations.

We cannot do this work without your prayers. Thank you for praying with us for the people we serve, the local believers we partner with, and the opportunities God gives us to share the hope of Christ.

To receive specific prayer requests and hear how God is answering them, sign up for our Brane Family Snippets below. We would love to have you join us in praying for West Africa.
