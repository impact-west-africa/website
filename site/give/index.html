Support IMPACT West Africa

Your giving supports the ministry as a whole, helping advance Gospel transformation through agricultural development, medical care, and humanitarian efforts across Senegal and West Africa.

IMPACT West Africa is supported entirely through the generous giving of churches and individuals who want to be part of what God is doing in West Africa. IMPACT West Africa, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that supports the ministry of Tom and Suja Brane.

When you give to IMPACT, you become part of this ministry.

Online Giving

Preferred

ACH / Bank Account

We encourage giving through ACH / bank account when possible because it is a convenient way to give and allows more of your gift to go directly toward ministry.

Online giving makes it easy to give one-time or recurring monthly gifts. You can securely give using your bank account (ACH), debit card, or credit card. Your gifts are tax-deductible.

Give Now
Why Choose ACH?+−

ACH is simply a way to give directly from your bank account instead of writing and mailing a check. You securely enter your bank account information, and your gift is automatically transferred to IMPACT West Africa according to the schedule you choose.

  • Convenient — Your gift is sent automatically, making it easy to set up recurring monthly support.
  • Consistent — Automatic giving helps you stay on track with your giving.
  • Lower cost — ACH costs just $0.30 per transaction, allowing more of your gift to go directly toward ministry.
  • Less administration — Online giving reduces the number of checks and cash contributions that must be received, recorded, deposited, and processed.

As a small, volunteer-led organization, reducing administrative work allows us to keep our U.S. operations lean and direct more of our resources toward ministry in West Africa.

Need help?+−

Here's how to give online:

  1. Click the Give Now button above. This opens our secure online giving page.
  2. Enter the amount you would like to give.
  3. Choose how often: one time, or a recurring schedule such as monthly, and pick a start date.
  4. Enter your email address or mobile number. You will receive a short code to confirm it’s you.
  5. Choose your payment method. For ACH, select Bank account and either connect your bank or enter your routing and account numbers. You can also use a debit or credit card.
  6. Review your gift and click Give. You’ll receive an email receipt.
  7. To change or cancel a recurring gift later, return to the giving page and sign in with the same email or mobile number.

Still having trouble, no problem. Email us at: info@impactwestafrica.org

Why Give

We believe generosity is a response to God’s generosity toward us. Everything we have comes from Him, and because of all He has done for us through Christ, we want to live open-handed lives, giving freely and sacrificially so that others can know the love of Christ and experience the hope we have found in Him.
