About us

Who we are, how we work, and the people who stand behind it.

Our partners

On the field we partner with CAMA Services, the relief and development branch of the Christian and Missionary Alliance, and with MedSend, which makes it possible for medical professionals to serve overseas.

Our story

Fourteen years in West Africa, and still learning what it means to stay.

We're Tom and Suja Brane. Tom grew up in Markle, Indiana; Suja grew up in Cincinnati. We have three children — Anna, Naomi, and Josiah — who have grown up between two worlds. We have served in West Africa for fourteen years.

Our work is based out of the Beersheba Development Farm, where Tom teaches Farming God's Way and Suja serves as a family medicine doctor at the farm's rural clinic. We are independently supported by individuals and churches.

Our hope is to use agriculture and medicine to meet real felt needs and plant seeds of hope in people's lives, making disciples of Jesus so that the gospel can make an IMPACT in West Africa.

West Africa Where we serve

Our strategy

We use agricultural development and medical care to meet real, felt needs — and out of that work come relationships built on trust and respect that lead to changed lives for Jesus.

Step one

Meet a real, felt need

Better harvests. Medical care that would otherwise be out of reach.

Step two

Build trust and respect

We stay, we return, and we show up in the hard moments alongside the people we serve.

Step three

Speak into lives about Jesus

So they have the same opportunity each of us has to accept Jesus as Lord and Savior.

Step four

Multiply through others

We disciple and equip farmers to train and teach in their own nations, so the work keeps growing beyond us.

Board of directors

The people who guide and hold this ministry accountable.

Tom Brane

Tom graduated from Cincinnati Bible College in 1992 with a B.S. in Bible and Christian Education, where he met his wife, Suja. After three years in youth ministry and ten years teaching at Christ Centered School in Harrison, Ohio, he and Suja moved to West Africa in 2011. He teaches and trains individuals in Farming God's Way across West Africa, addressing both food insecurity and spiritual poverty.

Brian Smith

Brian graduated from Cincinnati Bible College with degrees in Bible and Missions in 1989 and served Traders Point Christian Church for fifteen years as Facilities Director and Missions Minister. In 2011 he founded Agristewards, splitting his time between Lamb Farms in Lebanon, Indiana and leading Farming God's Way trainings worldwide. He has taught agriculture from the Bible in twenty-six countries.

Dennis McDaniel

Dennis brings more than forty years of accounting, financial management, and investor relations experience with large property-casualty insurance companies. He has served in various volunteer positions at White Oak Christian Church, including two decades as an elder. He and his wife Adele have supported the accounting and financial side of IMPACT West Africa since 2016.

Minta Berry

Minta holds an MBA from Xavier University. After thirty years owning a curriculum development and production company, she moved into international teaching. She spent nine years in West Africa teaching and mentoring Anna, Naomi, and Josiah, retiring in 2024, and continues to consult on their education.

Dean Heitkamp

Dean became a Christian at fifteen and graduated from Cincinnati Bible College in 1978. He spent twenty-five years in located ministry in Ohio, Georgia, Indiana, and Florida, and has stayed involved in church ministry and missions since. He and his wife Lisa attend LifeSpring Christian Church in Harrison, Ohio, and have four married children and twelve grandchildren.

Suja Brane

Dr. Suja Brane is a family medicine physician who has served in West Africa since 2012 in some of the world's most underserved communities. She cares for patients suffering from chronic and debilitating illnesses, people often marginalized and overlooked by the society around them. She sees medicine as a powerful avenue for sharing Christ's love and bringing holistic healing.
