Statement of faith

The essential beliefs that unite us.

IMPACT West Africa is a Christian mission organization committed to making Jesus Christ known and impacting the unreached in West Africa through agricultural development, medical care, and gospel transformation.

This statement sets out the essential beliefs that unite the IMPACT board, missionaries and ministry partners. It is not a substitute for Scripture, which alone is our final authority. On matters not addressed here, we extend grace and liberty to one another: in essentials, unity; in non-essentials, liberty; in all things, love.

1.The Bible

We believe the Bible, consisting of the Old and New Testaments, is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for our faith, life, and ministry.

2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21; Psalm 119:105

2.God

We believe there is one true and living God, the Creator and Sustainer of all things, who eternally exists in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4; Genesis 1:1; Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14

3.Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully human. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the right hand of the Father, and will return in power and glory.

John 1:1–14; Philippians 2:5–11; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Acts 1:9–11

4.Humanity and Sin

We believe all people are created in the image of God and possess inherent dignity and worth. We also believe that all have sinned and are separated from God, unable to save themselves.

Genesis 1:26–27; Romans 3:23; Romans 6:23

5.Salvation

We believe salvation is the free gift of God's grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone and not earned by works. Following the pattern of the New Testament, each person responds to the gospel by believing in Jesus as Lord and Savior, repenting of sin, confessing faith in Him, and being baptized by immersion, as Christ commanded, and then living as His disciple.

Ephesians 2:8–10; John 3:16; Acts 2:38; Romans 10:9–10; Romans 6:3–4

6.The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts the world of sin, gives new life to those who believe, dwells in every Christian, and empowers believers for holy living and service.

John 16:7–13; Acts 2:38; Romans 8:9–11; Galatians 5:22–25

7.The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, made up of all true believers. Christ commands His Church to gather for worship, prayer, teaching, fellowship and the Lord's Supper, and to go and make disciples of all nations.

Matthew 16:18; Acts 2:42; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26; Matthew 28:18–20

8.Christian Life and Service

We believe followers of Jesus are called to love God and their neighbors, live in obedience to His Word, grow in Christlikeness, and serve others with humility, compassion, and integrity. We are stewards of the resources and responsibilities God has entrusted to us and are called to care for people and His creation. We believe meeting physical needs and proclaiming the gospel belong together. Every act of service aims to point people to Jesus Christ and to strengthen local churches.

Matthew 5:16; Luke 4:18–19

9.Care for the Poor

We believe God has a special concern for the poor and that His people are to love their neighbors in word and deed. Following Christ, we seek to serve the poor as fellow image-bearers of God, affirming their dignity and walking alongside them in humility as we equip and empower them to overcome challenges and flourish through their God-given abilities. Recognizing that poverty is ultimately rooted in spiritual brokenness, we desire above all that they come to know Jesus and experience the abundant life found in Him.

Proverbs 19:17; Micah 6:8; James 2:14–17; 1 John 3:17–18; John 10:10; Matthew 25:40

10.The Return of Christ and Eternal Life

We believe Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly. There will be a resurrection of the dead and a final judgment. Those who belong to Christ will share in eternal life with Him.

John 14:1–3; John 5:28–29; Revelation 21:1–4

Our Ministry Commitment

IMPACT West Africa seeks to serve communities in West Africa by integrating agricultural development, medical care, and the proclamation of the gospel. We desire to meet real felt needs and to equip individuals and communities to come out of physical and spiritual poverty. We humbly depend on God's grace, the guidance of His Word, and the power of the Holy Spirit as we work toward lasting transformation in individuals, families, and communities.
